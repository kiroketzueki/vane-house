Privacy Policy

Vane House: Four Colors in the Rain · Last updated: 24 November 2026

Short version

This game collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party trackers.

What is stored, and where

Your progress, settings and trophies are saved in your own device’s browser storage (localStorage). That data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Clearing the app’s storage, or using “Erase” in Settings, removes it.

The game also keeps a copy of its own files on your device (a service-worker cache) so it can run offline.

Network requests

When online, the game may fetch its own files from the server that hosts it, so that updates can be delivered. It makes no requests to any other service.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone. It deals with mature family themes (see the in-game Content Note) and is not designed for young children.
